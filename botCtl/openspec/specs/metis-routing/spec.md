# metis-routing Specification

## Purpose

Owns Metis-based turn routing: the stage-1 decision (coarse intent plus joint
followup/new_topic/unrelated transitions), the stage-2 feature-category
decision for bio questions, and the fallback to the legacy embedding decoder.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Stage 1 decides intent and transitions in one request

With Metis routing enabled, each freshly decoded turn SHALL be sent to the
Metis decision endpoint as one request containing the user input, the focus
entity (name and type, if any) and whether the previous question was answered.
The request SHALL contain exactly one choice question over the 17 simplified
intents and three yes/no questions (followup, new_topic, unrelated). No text
SHALL be generated by the endpoint.

#### Scenario: Clear bio question

- **WHEN** the input is "Was frisst der Eisvogel?"
- **THEN** the choice answer is bio_frage with probability above 0.9

#### Scenario: Follow-up against a focused entity

- **WHEN** the previous question about the Eisvogel was answered and the
  input is "und wie schwer wird er?"
- **THEN** the followup answer is above 0.7 and the choice answer is bio_frage

#### Scenario: Topic switch away from the focus

- **WHEN** the focus is the Eisvogel and the input is "Wo lebt der Moorfrosch?"
- **THEN** the new_topic answer is above 0.7

### Requirement: Routing order applies the joint answers deterministically

The stage-1 answers SHALL be applied in a fixed order: smalltalk or
beleidigung with choice probability above 0.5 wins first (focus cleared);
then a non-bio intent routes to its existing machinery; then unrelated at or
above 0.8 routes to the fallback intent; then followup at or above 0.7 with a
focus entity present reuses the focused entity and skips the entity search;
otherwise a new_topic answer at or above 0.7 clears the focus and the entity
search runs as usual. When the chosen intent is not a bio question and its
probability is below 0.5, the turn SHALL offer intent options instead of
routing (legacy unsure path); a bio question stays in the bio flow even when
its probability is below 0.5, because the entity search resolves bare entity
names.

#### Scenario: Greeting despite high unrelated score

- **WHEN** the input is "Hallo!" and unrelated is 0.83
- **THEN** the turn routes to smalltalk, not to the fallback intent

#### Scenario: Weather question is not unrelated

- **WHEN** the input is "Wie wird das Wetter morgen in Karlsruhe?"
- **THEN** the turn routes to the wetter intent

#### Scenario: Unrelated question despite a focus entity

- **WHEN** the focus is the Reh and the input is "gibt es was zu essen"
  (choice bio_frage through focus anchoring, followup below 0.7)
- **THEN** the input is probed again without the focus and routes to the
  verpflegung intent

#### Scenario: Bare entity name decodes weakly but still runs the bio flow

- **WHEN** the input is "rheinaue" and the bio_frage probability is 0.33
- **THEN** the bio flow runs and the entity search resolves the Rheinaue
  instead of offering intent options

#### Scenario: Undecided transition falls through to the entity search

- **WHEN** the focus is a plant and the input names another plant
  (followup 0.44, new_topic 0.40)
- **THEN** the entity search runs and resolves the newly named entity

#### Scenario: Gibberish offers intent options

- **WHEN** the input is "xzqgrumpf blorps" and the fallback choice
  probability is 0.35
- **THEN** intent options are offered instead of a fallback answer

### Requirement: Stage 2 decides the feature category for bio answers

For a bio question with a resolved entity, Metis SHALL be asked once for the
feature category (verhalten, eigenschaften, beschreibung). The answer SHALL
select a column group from the category map; the reply SHALL contain the
merged data of the group's filled columns plus a media attachment (audio for
verhalten, otherwise an image) when present.

#### Scenario: Behaviour question returns merged behaviour data

- **WHEN** the question is "Was frisst der Eisvogel?" and the Eisvogel is
  resolved
- **THEN** the answer contains the filled columns of verhalten and an audio
  link when one exists

#### Scenario: Latin name asks for description

- **WHEN** the question is "Was ist Malus sylvestris?"
- **THEN** the category beschreibung is chosen and the answer contains the
  name and classification columns

### Requirement: Undecided category answers with overview and options

When the top two category probabilities differ by less than 0.10, or the
category confidence is below 0.35, the reply SHALL be a compact overview of
all filled categories plus 2-3 options drawn randomly from the entity's
actually filled feature columns.

#### Scenario: Vague question triggers options

- **WHEN** the question is "Was gibt es Interessantes über die
  Gelbbauchunke zu sehen?" and confidence is below 0.35
- **THEN** the reply contains merged data from all categories and offers
  feature options

### Requirement: Disabled or failed Metis routing falls back to the legacy decoder

Metis routing SHALL be used only when enabled in the configuration. When
disabled, or when a Metis request fails (network error, non-200 after retry,
timeout), the turn SHALL be decoded by the existing embedding decoder without
user-visible error, and the legacy bio feature flow SHALL apply.

#### Scenario: Flag off keeps current behaviour

- **WHEN** METIS_ROUTING is false
- **THEN** intents are decoded by the embedding decoder exactly as before

#### Scenario: Metis outage falls back silently

- **WHEN** the Metis endpoint answers 503 twice
- **THEN** the turn is decoded by the legacy decoder and no error reaches
  the client
